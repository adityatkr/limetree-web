Frequently Asked Questions

Is it cheaper to book directly on this website?

Yes. Booking direct always gets you our best available rate, plus perks like free cancellation and offers that aren't available on third-party platforms.

What's the difference between a hotel room and a serviced apartment?

A serviced apartment gives you a separate bedroom, living room, and full kitchen — built for longer, independent stays. A hotel room is ideal for shorter visits with daily housekeeping and on-site dining.

What is the check-in and check-out time?

Standard check-in is 2:00 PM and check-out is 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in and late check-out can be arranged subject to availability.

Which cities do you have properties in?

Lime Tree Hotels operates 30+ properties across Gurgaon, Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Jaipur, Goa, and Vrindavan — including hotels, serviced apartments, a villa, and banquet halls.
