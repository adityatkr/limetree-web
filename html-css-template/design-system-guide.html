Every color, type style, component, and content pattern used across limetreehotels — in one place, so any change to the look of the site can be made consistently. This mirrors the live tokens in src/app/globals.css exactly; nothing here is aspirational.

FAQ Accordion

Native <details>/<summary> — no JS. Appears on the homepage, every property-type page (hotels, serviced apartments), and the dedicated /faq page, always ending in a "Still have questions?" WhatsApp/contact prompt.

Is it cheaper to book directly on this website? ⌄

Yes — booking direct always gets you our best available rate, plus free cancellation.

What is the check-in and check-out time? ⌄

Standard check-in is 2:00 PM, check-out is 12:00 PM.

Page Section Patterns

Recurring section shapes reused across every landing page (hotels, serviced-apartments, medical-tourism, corporate, homepage).

Hero
stone-900 background photo at 20–30% opacity, white h1 with a primary-400 highlighted phrase, one primary CTA + one outline CTA. Full-bleed, py-28.
Eyebrow + Heading
Centered or left-aligned pair: small uppercase primary-600 label, then a Playfair Display h2 directly beneath. Used to open almost every section.
Alternating background rhythm
Sections alternate bg-white / bg-stone-50 down the page so adjacent sections stay visually distinct without borders.
"Still have questions?" block
Closes every FAQ section: primary-50 panel, centered heading, Contact Support + WhatsApp Us buttons side by side.
13

Content & Voice

Conventions that keep copy and data consistent as new pages get added.

CTA wording"Book Now" for anything that leads to booking. "View All FAQs" / "Contact Support" for secondary links. Never "View Details," "Learn More," or "Explore" on a property card.
Destination orderGurgaon, Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Jaipur, Goa, Vrindavan — this exact sequence in nav, footer, homepage, /destinations, and the search widget.
Serviced apartment groupingListings group by unit size: 1 BHK → 2 BHK → 3 BHK → Studio → Other (only if something doesn't match).
Price displayHeadline price = the cheapest bookable room/unit at that property (shown as "From ₹X/night"), never an average.
FAQ toneDirect answers, no hedging — "Yes, X is included" rather than "X may be available."
14

Icons

In the Next.js app: lucide-react, stroke-based, sized 11–22px depending on context (11–13px inline with small text, 16–18px for feature icons, 20–22px for nav/menu controls). In standalone HTML templates (no npm access): hand-drawn inline SVG copies of the same lucide paths, same stroke-width of 2.
